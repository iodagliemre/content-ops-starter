## SaaS vs Ownership (mevcut)
H2: Right Model, Right Time. Sub: SaaS is a tool, ownership is a strategy.
SaaS: quick standard digitization / low initial cost market test / small team, provider handles maintenance / no deep customization need yet / short-term or seasonal.
Ownership: full control in critical processes / regulation-heavy, data sovereignty mandatory / institutional memory in own system / long-term investment, roadmap in your hands / industry-specific workflows beyond platform limits.
Kapanış: Doveify stands on the ownership side — because we believe businesses should not hand over strategic control of their most critical processes to another provider.
