# Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

## Introduction
Doveify ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

## Information We Collect
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, clinic name when you register
- **Usage Data:** How you interact with our platform
- **Patient Data:** Stored securely and only accessible by you

## How We Use Your Information
- To provide and maintain our service
- To notify you about changes to our service
- To provide customer support
- To detect and prevent technical issues

## Data Security
Your data is encrypted and stored on secure EU servers. We are fully GDPR compliant.

## Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Export your data

## Contact Us
For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: **hello@doveify.io**
